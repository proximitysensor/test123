Let’s talk about your news diet. In 2015, Columbia University researchers found that 59% of social media links are shared without being clicked.. That’s right: most people share headlines without reading the article — curious behavior, isn’t it? You might pride yourself on being more discerning, recognizing when headlines are designed to trigger emotional reactions. Yet how often do you still click those provocative headlines, even while thinking, “I see what they’re doing here”?
